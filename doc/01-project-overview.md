# Suqly MVP

## Project Overview

Suqly is a Progressive Web Application (PWA) that enables small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) to create their own digital storefronts and manage products and customer orders from a single platform.

Instead of relying solely on WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, or Instagram to sell products, businesses can showcase their products through a dedicated online storefront while managing inventory and orders through a centralized dashboard.

Customers can browse products from multiple businesses, add products to a store-specific Zembil (shopping cart), place orders, and track their order status in real time.

The MVP focuses on providing a simple and efficient order management system rather than a complete e-commerce solution. Payments and delivery are handled outside the platform, allowing Suqly to concentrate on helping businesses organize their sales workflow.

Designed with scalability in mind, the MVP establishes the foundation for future features such as online payments, delivery management, analytics, AI-powered recommendations, and multi-store support.

---

## Problem Statement

Many Ethiopian small businesses sell products through messaging applications and social media platforms such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Facebook, and Instagram.

While these platforms help businesses reach customers, they do not provide tools for managing products, tracking orders, or organizing business operations. As a result, business owners frequently answer the same customer questions, manually track inventory, and manage orders through chat conversations, making it difficult to stay organized.

Customers also have no simple way to browse available products or track the status of their orders after placing them.

Suqly addresses these challenges by providing each business with a digital storefront and a centralized platform for managing products and customer orders.

## Target Users

### Business Owners

Small and medium-sized businesses that want to display products online and manage customer orders efficiently.

Examples include:

- Clothing stores
- Shoe stores
- Electronics shops
- Grocery stores
- Cosmetic shops
- Bakeries
- Furniture stores
- Home-based businesses
- Social media sellers

### Customers

Individuals who want to browse products, place orders, and track order progress from different businesses through a single platform.

---

## Main Features

### Business Owners

- Register and sign in
- Create and manage a store
- Add, edit, publish, and delete products
- Manage inventory
- Receive new order notifications
- Update order status
- View completed and cancelled orders

### Customers

- Register and sign in
- Browse products from different stores
- View product details
- Add products to a store-specific Zembil
- Place orders
- Track order status
- View order history

---

## What Done Looks Like (MVP)

The MVP is considered complete when:

- Users can register as either a Business Owner or Customer.
- Business Owners can create a store and publish products.
- Customers can browse products and place orders through the Zembil.
- Business Owners receive incoming orders and update their status.
- Customers receive status updates and can track their orders.
- Completed and cancelled orders are stored in history.
- The application is fully responsive and functions as a Progressive Web Application (PWA).
